## Locked design summary (final changes only)

- **Metal:** brushed/aged **brass** (sconces, dome lamps, slim inlays, edges, hardware).
- **Soft neutrals:** bone/cream linen bedding; plush wool rug.
- **Hardware:** handleless groove / push-to-open, with slim brass edges.
