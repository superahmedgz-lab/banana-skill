## PART 2 — The Four Design Options

### OPTION 1 — "Morning Sage" · Soft Sage Green Japandi
1. **Concept:** the lightest, most spa-like scheme — airy Scandinavian calm with Japanese warmth.
2. **Mood / direction:** serene, uncluttered, restorative; light woods and muted green.
3. **Palette:** Walls Summer Snow 1928 · Wood = **Sand Oak on all joinery** · Headboard **muted dusty sage** · Bedding warm white + oatmeal, one sage layer · Accents sage + warm sand · Metal **matte black** (slim) or minimal aged brass.
4. **Materials:** sand-oak veneer; sage **bouclé or matte linen** headboard; oatmeal **linen** curtains; **wool/jute** rug in oatmeal; stoneware + pale ceramic décor; fine **vertical reeded** sand-oak on the TV cladding; keep the sandblasting mirror (soft, light-bouncing).
5. **Furniture treatment:** TV cladding = reeded Sand Oak; Dressers A&B, vanity, side tables = Sand Oak, groove/handleless; glass cabinet = Sand Oak frame + clear glass + 3000K internal LED; bed = freestanding base in oatmeal linen, fixed sage headboard.
6. **Lighting (3000K):** ambient recessed downlights + perimeter cove; task = slim reading wall-lights flanking the headboard; accent = 3000K LED in glass cabinet + a soft graze on the reeded cladding.
7. **Styling:** warm-white duvet + oatmeal flat sheet + sage lumbar + 2 linen euros; chunky-knit throw in cream; one large leafy plant (bird of paradise) + a small trailing plant on a side table; one calm abstract-landscape artwork in sand/sage; 2–3 ceramic objects only.
8. **Why it works:** the light Sand Oak is the best bridge to the grey floor; sage + off-white + warm 3000K light is textbook serene Japandi; minimal palette keeps it uncluttered.

### OPTION 2 — "Terracotta Warmth" · Warm Terracotta Japandi
1. **Concept:** earthy, sunset-warm Japandi with a grounding dark-wood anchor.
2. **Mood / direction:** cozy, intimate, natural; clay tones and layered woods.
3. **Palette:** Walls Summer Snow 1928 · Wood = **Sand Oak main + Espresso Walnut on the TV cladding feature & glass cabinet** · Headboard **terracotta/clay** · Bedding cream + warm sand + rust throw · Accents terracotta, caramel, espresso · Metal **aged/antique brass**.
4. **Materials:** espresso reeded TV cladding; sand-oak dressers/vanity/side tables; **terracotta velvet (or velvet-linen)** headboard; warm clay/oatmeal **linen** curtains; **wool** rug in terracotta + sand; travertine/clay-stone décor; dried branch or pampas.
5. **Furniture treatment:** TV cladding + glass cabinet = Espresso Walnut (hero contrast); Dressers, vanity, side tables = Sand Oak; brass hairline accents on the glass cabinet; handleless elsewhere.
6. **Lighting (3000K):** cove + recessed ambient; warm graze on the espresso cladding to show grain; brass-toned bedside reading lights; 3000K display-cabinet LED.
7. **Styling:** cream bedding + terracotta velvet lumbar + caramel knit throw; 2 textured euros; olive or rubber plant; one warm earthy artwork; travertine tray + 2 ceramics.
8. **Why it works:** terracotta + warm woods come alive under 3000K; the espresso cladding gives a premium anchor and ties to your dark sample; warm textiles offset the grey floor.

### OPTION 3 — "Quiet Greige" · Neutral Beige/Greige Japandi + subtle green
1. **Concept:** the most timeless, ultra-calm, safest-with-the-grey-floor scheme.
2. **Mood / direction:** quiet, sophisticated, layered neutrals with a whisper of sage.
3. **Palette:** Walls Summer Snow 1928 · Wood = **Sand Oak throughout** · Headboard **greige / mushroom** (optional sage piping) · Bedding warm white + greige + a soft sage cushion · Accents greige, mushroom, soft sage · Metal **blackened/antique brass** or matte black.
4. **Materials:** sand-oak joinery; greige **linen or wool-blend** headboard; oatmeal/greige **linen** curtains; natural **wool** rug; stone + ceramic décor; eucalyptus/olive stems.
5. **Furniture treatment:** all joinery Sand Oak, handleless/groove; glass cabinet Sand Oak + clear glass + 3000K LED; freestanding base in greige linen + greige fixed headboard.
6. **Lighting (3000K):** cove + recessed ambient; subtle reeded-cladding graze; bedside reading lights; 3000K cabinet LED.
7. **Styling:** layered warm-white + greige bedding + one sage accent + linen euros; mushroom throw; eucalyptus in a stoneware vase; one soft neutral artwork; 2–3 quiet objects.
8. **Why it works:** greige + sand oak agree beautifully with both the grey floor and the off-white walls — the most cohesive, low-risk result; the sage accent stops it from feeling flat.

### OPTION 4 — "Hotel Noir Japandi" · Premium hotel-inspired (uses your dark sample fully)
1. **Concept:** boutique-hotel Japandi — dark wood, velvet, and brass, exactly in the spirit of your 3D render.
2. **Mood / direction:** dramatic, refined, premium; strong but controlled contrast.
3. **Palette:** Walls Summer Snow 1928 · Wood = **Espresso Walnut as the hero** (TV cladding + Dressers + glass cabinet + side tables), Sand Oak only as interior/accent · Headboard **deep sage velvet** (or terracotta velvet) · Bedding crisp white + greige + velvet accent · Accents espresso, sage velvet, **brushed aged brass (hero metal)** · 
4. **Materials:** espresso reeded cladding; full-height glass display cabinet with **brass-edge + 3000K LED**; **velvet** headboard; heavy greige **linen** curtains; large low-pile **wool** rug (charcoal-sand); marble/travertine tray; one sculptural lamp.
5. **Furniture treatment:** Espresso Walnut hero joinery, handleless/push-to-open; brass hairline frames on glass cabinet; sand-oak cabinet interiors for contrast.
6. **Lighting (3000K):** layered + dimmable; cove for ambient; brass picture/wall lights; 3000K graze on espresso grain; 3000K cabinet LED. Dimmers essential to keep dark wood from going heavy.
7. **Styling:** crisp white bedding + sage velvet lumbar + greige knit throw + 2 euros; one large statement plant; a single large moody-calm artwork; marble tray + sculptural ceramic; keep surfaces minimal.
8. **Why it works:** dark wood + brass + velvet reads as premium hotel; the grey floor becomes a sophisticated base; **only works if walls stay light and lighting stays 3000K + dimmable** so it feels warm, not heavy. This option uses your Espresso Walnut sample exactly as drawn in the 3D.

## PART 4 — Comparison & Recommendation

| | Wood used | Headboard | Best for | Risk with grey floor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 Morning Sage** | Sand Oak (all) | Dusty sage bouclé | Lightest, most serene | Lowest — light wood bridges grey best |
| **2 Terracotta Warmth** | Sand Oak + Espresso cladding | Terracotta velvet | Cozy, warm, characterful | Low — warm textiles offset grey |

| **4 Hotel Noir** | Espresso Walnut (hero) | Sage/terracotta velvet | Dramatic, premium hotel | Medium — needs light walls + dimmed 3000K |

### My recommendation (best 1–2)
- **Primary: Option 3 "Quiet Greige" with a sage accent** — it is the most cohesive with your *fixed* grey floor + off-white walls, fully Japandi, timeless, and low-risk. Sand Oak warms the grey, greige + a sage whisper keeps it calm and current.
- **Close second / "wow" pick: Option 4 "Hotel Noir Japandi"** — if you want the premium hotel drama of your 3D render and to use the Espresso Walnut sample as the hero. Choose this only if you commit to **dimmable 3000K** lighting and keep the walls light, so the dark wood reads warm and rich, not heavy.
- **Hybrid I'd genuinely suggest:** Option 3 base (sand-oak dressers, greige headboard) **+ the Espresso Walnut reeded TV cladding panel from Option 2/4** as a single dark feature. You get the calm cohesion of greige with one premium dark focal point — the best of both, and it matches the drawn feature wall.

### Non-negotiables I kept
- Floor and 3000K lighting unchanged in every option.
- Layout per the Wood Co plan (no relocations).
- No colours that clash with Summer Snow walls or the grey floor.
- Japandi restraint — no over-decoration.
- The **one change I urge:** switch the plan's integrated **4000K LED to 3000K**.
